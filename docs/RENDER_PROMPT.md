# Image generation prompt

For visualising the object. Not a spec — the generator will get proportions
wrong. Written in English; models handle it better.

## Main prompt

> A handheld retro-futurist tarot device, the exact size and proportions of a
> tarot deck in its box — tall, narrow, and thick in the hand. Three-quarter
> view, tilted slightly toward the viewer, resting on a dark wooden surface.
>
> The body is turned walnut and aged brass: a warm brass frame with wooden side
> panels, softly worn at the corners, brushed rather than polished. Visible
> countersunk screws. The look of a 1970s scientific instrument rather than a
> gadget.
>
> The front face is dominated by a large matte electronic-paper display —
> **non-emissive, paper-white, absolutely no backlight or glow**, showing a few
> lines of dark serif text. It reads like printed card stock set into the frame.
>
> Both long edges of the case are chamfered at 45 degrees, and each bevel is a
> translucent strip lit from within by a vertical row of eight small warm amber
> points of light, evenly spaced, glowing softly through frosted material. One
> of them is brighter than the rest.
>
> At the two top corners sit two knurled brass gear wheels, half-sunk into the
> body so their toothed edges protrude past the top edge — clearly meant to be
> turned by the index fingers. Between them, a row of fine milled slots for a
> microphone.
>
> Warm low-key lighting from one side, deep shadows, shallow depth of field.
> Brass catches the light along its edges. Amber glow reflects faintly on the
> wood. Quiet, occult, precise. Photographic, not illustrated.

## What generators get wrong here

Put these in a negative prompt or repeat them:

- **glowing screen, backlit display, LCD, blue light** — e-ink emits nothing
- **wide, flat, tablet-like** — it must read as thick and hand-sized, a deck of
  cards, not a phone
- keyboards, many buttons, exposed circuit boards, wires
- neon, cyberpunk, purple-and-teal, sci-fi HUD
- tarot card illustrations printed on the body

## Variants worth trying

**Held in hand** — add: *held in both hands, gripped from the sides, index
fingers resting on the two brass wheels at the top corners, thumbs steadying the
body.* Shows the grip, which is the whole idea.

**In the dark** — add: *photographed in near-darkness, the amber edge lights the
only source, faintly illuminating the wood grain and a fanned-out tarot spread on
the table beside it.*

**Flat lay** — add: *shot from directly above on black velvet, beside a stack of
worn tarot cards and a brass key.* Better for showing the face; loses the
thickness.

## Real dimensions, if the generator will take them
76 × 41 × 126 mm. Screen active area 47 × 81 mm. Eight lights at 10 mm pitch
running the height of the screen.
