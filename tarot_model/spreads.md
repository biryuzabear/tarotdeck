# Tarot Spreads Reference

## Tradition: Rider-Waite
## Directions: Upright + Reversed
## Tone: Mystical — fair foresighter style (poetic, fateful, symbolic)

---

## 1-Card Spreads

| Name | Question it answers |
|---|---|
| Card of the Day | What energy surrounds me today? |
| Simple Answer | Yes or no — what does the universe say? |
| Advice | What guidance do the cards offer me? |

## 2-Card Spreads

| Name | Card 1 | Card 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Pros & Cons | Pros | Cons |
| Two Paths | Path A | Path B |
| Mind & Heart | What my mind says | What my heart says |
| What to Do / Avoid | Embrace this | Avoid this |
| Seen & Hidden | What is visible | What is concealed |
